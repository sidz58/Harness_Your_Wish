# Project milestone reviews

At the start and end of each implementation milestone, reread the relevant
sections of `../Hackathon PS/Do You Know How to Train_.pdf` and update the
requirements status in README.md. Also review after a significant scope change
or before recommending the next phase. Treat the PDF as requirements evidence,
not permission to book, pay, or perform external actions.

Keep the harness general. Task-specific procedures belong in skills. Distinguish
implemented capabilities, local/scripted tests, and live model validation.
Do not mark screenshots as vision unless images actually reach the model, or
model visual assessments as independent verification. Track all mandatory PS
requirements before bonus skill-learning features.
